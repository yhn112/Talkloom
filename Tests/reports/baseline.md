# ASR baseline

Measured on 2026-08-25 with `google/gemini-3.7-flash` through OpenRouter Chat Completions,
zero-data-retention routing, the committed prompt and no VAD. All audio was generated by
`scripts/generate-asr-smoke-fixtures.sh`; these results establish the transport comparator,
not real-meeting quality.

segment timestamps. The provider's returned segments stayed within each speech file's trusted
duration on this run.

## Silence and response variance

Three identical five-second silence requests produced two hallucinated, invalid payloads and
one valid empty result:

| Attempt | Result | Runtime | Peak memory | Provider cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | At least two segments; segment 1 ended at 8.42 s | 5.23 s wall time | 17.8 MiB | unavailable after rejection |
| 2 | At least two segments; segment 1 ended at 8.44 s | 10.55 s wall time | 18.3 MiB | unavailable after rejection |
| 3 | Empty segment array | 0.616 xRT | 18.0 MiB | $0.000304 |

The two out-of-duration results are hallucinations regardless of their unavailable text: pure
silence contains no valid segment. This reproduced behavior is why the no-VAD path remains an
evaluation comparator only and why Stage 2 requires VAD before production requests.

The same request variance appeared on Russian speech: the first of three identical requests
was rejected as an invalid segment before detailed validation diagnostics existed; the next
two produced the exact normalized transcript at 0.740 and 0.828 xRT. A single successful run
therefore does not establish response-schema stability.
